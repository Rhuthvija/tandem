# Tandem roadmap

Ideas that came up while building Tandem, saved for after the hackathon.

## Next
- **Paper-cut journal UI**: paper textures, torn-note cards, pen-stroke ticks, folding sheets, otter expressions (celebrate, sleepy, thinking) with stop-motion style animation
- **Day map**: today as a visual path; new and changed tasks slide in and glow, with Tandem's note pinned next to the change
- **Laptop dashboard** with a focus timer, plus a floating mini window (Next up + timer)
- **Push notifications** when the app is closed (installable app + service worker)
- **Weekly / monthly recap**, frequency chosen by the user
- Smarter completion questions for counted tasks ("how many of the 50 did you get through?")

## Later
- Native iOS / Android app with home-screen widgets
- Google sign-in and sync across devices
- Tandem speaking replies out loud
- Training-wheels mode: over weeks the user leads the huddle and Tandem just asks questions
- Optional money goals as plannable tasks
- Daily growth skills (e.g. a short talk on one topic a day)
